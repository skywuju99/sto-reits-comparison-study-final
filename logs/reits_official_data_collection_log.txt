2022  -31.34%
2023   +7.66%
2024  +12.83%
2025   -7.43%

Interpretation:
The U.S. Residential sector is used as the primary benchmark because
the RealT sample consists of U.S. residential rental properties.

The official REIT values are total returns including price changes and
distributions. STO observed capital returns exclude rental distributions.
The two measures are not interpreted as directly comparable performance
measures.
