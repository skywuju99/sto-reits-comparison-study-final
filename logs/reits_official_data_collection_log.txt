3. USE IN THE THESIS
--------------------

The FTSE Nareit U.S. Real Estate Index Series Residential sector is used
as the primary REIT benchmark because the RealT empirical sample consists
of U.S. residential rental RealTokens.

The FTSE EPRA Nareit Global Real Estate Index Series Global USD Total Return
is used only as a supplementary benchmark.

The main comparison uses complete calendar years from 2022 through 2025.
The 2026 partial-year STO results are not directly compared with these
complete-calendar-year REIT returns.


4. INTERPRETATION
-----------------

The STO observed secondary-market capital-return measure excludes rental
distributions, whereas the official REIT total-return measures include
price changes and distributions.

The market-price-reflected STO composite-return measure is an exploratory
proxy and is not identical to an official REIT total-return measure.

The comparison is descriptive and does not establish direct performance
superiority.


5. DATA AVAILABILITY AND LIMITATION
-----------------------------------

The complete underlying FTSE/Nareit proprietary datasets and machine-readable
benchmark data are not included in this repository.

This repository records the benchmark definition, scope, currency, return
type, source location, access date, and analytical role used in the thesis.

Researchers seeking to reproduce the REIT comparison should obtain the
corresponding benchmark observations independently from the official
Nareit/FTSE sources.
